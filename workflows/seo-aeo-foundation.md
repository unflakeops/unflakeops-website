# SEO and AEO foundation

## Release objective

Give search engines and answer engines a clear, consistent account of who UnflakeOps is, which audience it serves and what the first offer covers. Add one useful, indexable guide based on Muhammad's LinkedIn posts without presenting the working offer as proven product-market fit.

## Search intent map

| Intent | Primary page | Working query themes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand the offer | Home | Power Platform automation for UK charities; charity reporting automation |
| Investigate the problem | First insight | automate charity reporting; manual funder reporting; trustee report workflow |
| Assess the approach | First insight and pilot section | what to map before automation; Power Platform charity reporting; reporting workflow pilot |
| Check credibility and terms | Home, privacy and terms | UnflakeOps; Muhammad Qureshi UnflakeOps; Wadud Ltd trading as UnflakeOps |

These are working themes derived from the active offer, public charity reporting discussions and this week's LinkedIn posts. They are not search-volume claims. Search Console data should replace assumptions once enough impressions are available.

## Implemented

- Consistent `www.unflakeops.com` canonical host in robots and sitemap output.
- Organization, Person, WebSite and Service JSON-LD on the home page.
- Indexable Insights hub with CollectionPage and ItemList JSON-LD.
- First article with Article and visible FAQ JSON-LD.
- Muhammad Qureshi authorship and LinkedIn identity on the article.
- Descriptive titles, descriptions, canonical URLs and social metadata.
- Internal links from the primary navigation and footer.
- Updated sitemap priorities and new Insights URLs.
- Visible sourcing and a clear distinction between working method and client results.

## Measurement after production release

1. Verify the `www` domain property in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
2. Submit `https://www.unflakeops.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. Request indexing for the home page, Insights hub and first article.
4. Check indexing, impressions, queries and click-through rate weekly for the first eight weeks.
5. Record enquiries and qualified charity conversations separately from search visits.
6. Use real queries and prospect questions to choose the second article.

Do not add analytics until the chosen tool, consent requirement and privacy-policy wording have been agreed.
