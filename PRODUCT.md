# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Engineering leaders, ML/platform teams, and founders shipping AI features (RAG systems, LLM
pipelines, data platforms) who are burned by unreliable, non-deterministic, or low-quality
outputs. They arrive skeptical and technically literate; they want evidence of rigor, not hype.

## Product Purpose
UnflakeOps is a consultancy/marketing site for an AI-reliability and data-maturity practice:
RAG evaluation, hallucination control, deterministic LLM outputs, LLM-ready data, and data
pipelines. The site's job is to convey credibility and senior-led rigor, then convert to a
discovery call / contact. Success = qualified leads booked or captured.

## Brand Personality
Precise, senior, calm. Three words: **rigorous, steady, engineered.** The voice is an expert
who measures before claiming. It should feel like instrumentation — a reliability console —
not a generic SaaS landing page.

## Anti-references
- Generic dark-SaaS gradient landing pages (purple-blue glow, hero-metric template).
- AI-slop marks: a sparkline-in-a-rounded-tile, an up-and-to-the-right chart, a large rounded
  icon above every heading.
- Costume "developer" mono everywhere; eyebrow kickers above every section.

## Design Principles
1. **Practice what you preach** — the site itself must feel reliable, fast, and precise.
2. **Instrumentation over decoration** — visuals read as measurement (steady signals, evals).
3. **Show the un-flaking** — the core idea is jitter resolving into steadiness; the brand mark
   should carry that concept, not a generic chart.
4. **Senior restraint, not timidity** — one committed accent (green) on a deep surface; no hedging.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 on the deep surface. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks.
Brand mark must stay legible at 16px and not rely on color alone (works in monochrome).
